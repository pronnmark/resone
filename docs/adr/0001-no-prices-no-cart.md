# 0001 — No prices and no cart

Date: 2026-09-29. Status: accepted (studio ruling).

**Context.** The deck carries two prices (59 000 and 175 000 FCFA). In the memos the studio
said a shop feel *"could feel cheap"* and preferred *"if interested, contact us"*.

**Decision.** No price appears on the site. Priced pieces read *sur demande*. Each piece
in the lightbox has an email link that names the piece, and the contact card invites
custom requests.

**Consequences.** The site cannot sell directly, by design. Prices stay in the deck, which
we did not edit. Any new copy for the inquiry lines needs the studio's approval.
