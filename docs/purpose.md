# Purpose — who we are, what we are doing, and why

Read this before deciding what the site should do. It answers the question every
change should start from. Sources: the studio's Canva deck and its voice memos of
2026-09-26. Where the sources are silent, this file says so.

## Who we are

**Résone** is a textile-art and artistic-direction studio in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire, run by
the sisters **Omara Ré** and **Inès Ré**. They are Franco-Mauritian, work by hand in pairs
(*quatre-mains*), and describe themselves as *une entreprise de création et de conseil
artistique*. They were incubated in the Ministry of Culture's Créative Côte d'Ivoire
programme in 2026. The full profile is in [`README.md`](../README.md).

We (this repo) are the people building their portfolio site. We are not the studio, and
we do not speak for it: copy and rulings come from them.

## What we are doing

Building **resone.africa**, a four-page digital portfolio — Portfolio, Atelier, À propos,
Contact. It shows three bodies of work (ombres chinoises, HANOKA, patchwork), one
masterpiece first, and leads a visitor to one action: *write to us*.

It is a gallery, not a shop. There is no cart and no price on the page.

## Why

The studio's own reasons, in their words:

1. **To be seen as artists, not as a boutique.** *"If you make it like an internet shop, it
   could feel cheap."* A pre-selling look would undercut the work and the price of a
   custom piece.
2. **To have a home for the work they keep having to explain.** Each new style is protected
   as a new model at about 10 000 FCFA, so the site is also the public record of their
   signature styles.
3. **To be reachable for commissions.** The named example is the made-to-measure apron for a
   wine house. The site's job is to make an interested person write to them.
4. **To look real.** A visible watermark gives *"an instant feeling that this site is
   copied."* Image quality delayed the whole project.
5. **To reach a phone.** About 80% of visits will be on a phone and will scroll one page.

## Who it is for

The memos never say. This is my reading, from the deck, and should be confirmed:

- people who commission custom textile pieces (the wine-house apron is the one example);
- hosts looking for a lit piece for an evening (*Ivresse écarlate* is pitched that way);
- institutions and partners, given the incubation and the partners page the studio floated.

## What success looks like

Not stated by the studio. A reasonable working test, to confirm with them: a visitor
understands in one scroll what the three styles are, remembers the masterpiece, and knows
how to write to Omara or Inès about a piece or a custom order.

## What is decided, and what is not

Decided (see [`adr/`](./adr/)): no prices; four pages; filter by style; gallery cut from the
Canva-hosted files. Not decided: English copy, a partners section, the newsletter, the
grouping of a few unmapped pieces, and the day/night slider. All wait on the studio;
the list is in [`../requirements-2026-09-26.md`](../requirements-2026-09-26.md).
